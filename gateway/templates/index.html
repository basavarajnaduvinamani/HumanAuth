Verify your identity

Three quick checks: a live selfie, a photo of your ID document, and a short voice sample. Each is scored by its own model, then combined into one decision.

Privacy

Your selfie, document and voice are sent only to this server, scored in memory and discarded. Nothing is saved to disk or logged, and responses are marked not to be cached. This is a research prototype from a student project, not a certified identity service: do not use real ID documents you are not entitled to share.
